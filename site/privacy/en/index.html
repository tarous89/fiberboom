Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how Fiberboom handles personal information when you visit fiberboom.com or contact us.

Who is responsible

Fiberboom is responsible for the personal information described here. Our address is Svanevej 22, 2400 København, Denmark. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@fiberboom.com.

Information processed and why

When you browse, technical information such as your IP address, browser information, requested URL, time of request and security signals may be processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, to deliver the website, prevent abuse and investigate faults. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) where your enquiry concerns steps you ask us to take before a contract, or otherwise our legitimate interest in answering correspondence under Article 6(1)(f). Please avoid sending sensitive health information.

Beta analytics and browser storage

During invited beta testing, we collect analytics on the basis of consent obtained through your invitation before participation. The on-site notice is informational; dismissing it does not grant or withdraw consent. To withdraw, email privacy@fiberboom.com and stop participating until tracking has been disabled for your visits.

We record pages visited and their sequence, visit times, scroll milestones, visible-tab time up to ten minutes per page view, button clicks, flavour and pack selections, and checkout intent. We also record referring website hostnames and campaign tags in links. We do not record arbitrary form input, payment details, session video, or keystrokes. Restock signups are described separately below. Page URLs are stored without query strings except the campaign tags described above.

We use local storage entries fb_visitor and fb_session to recognise the same browser and group activity into visits. The browser identifier expires after 90 days without a visit; a new session begins after 30 minutes without visible-page activity. Identifiers are random and are not names or email addresses. Clearing storage or using another browser breaks this connection. If browser storage is unavailable, identifiers last only for the current page.

Events are stored in Cloudflare D1 and removed by daily cleanup after 90 days, subject to provider backup retention. Administrators use a separate secure fb_admin cookie lasting up to eight hours. For abuse prevention, short-lived request limits use a keyed hash derived from an IP address; raw IP addresses are not saved in the analytics database. Cloudflare may process IP addresses for hosting and security.

The beta is intended for invited participants who have agreed to the described tracking. Consent records are maintained through the invitation process. We will introduce a public-site consent workflow before opening analytics testing to visitors who have not consented.

Restock emails and selected offers

When you click Checkout, we save the offer you selected: page version, flavour, pack size, displayed price and currency, delivery market, time and campaign tags. This is linked to the browser and visit identifiers described above. If you submit the restock form, we attach your email address and signup time to that same offer record so we can notify you about availability and send the advertised 30% discount offer. Submitting the form gives consent for those emails; it does not place an order or authorize payment. We do not add you to unrelated marketing lists.

You can withdraw consent or request removal by emailing privacy@fiberboom.com. These offer and email records are stored in Cloudflare D1 and deleted after 90 days by daily cleanup unless removed sooner at your request. The restock email is not sent automatically by this website; Fiberboom will use the signup list to contact interested visitors.

Who receives information

We use Cloudflare for website hosting, content delivery and security, and IONOS for company email. These providers may process information to supply their services. We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We do not sell your personal information.

International processing

Our providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Where a restricted transfer occurs, applicable safeguards must be in place, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request information about relevant safeguards by emailing us. See also Cloudflare’s privacy information.

How long information is kept

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to answer and follow up your enquiry, and longer only where necessary to meet a legal obligation or deal with a dispute. Technical information is retained according to the hosting and security services in use and the period needed for security and troubleshooting. Retention depends on the purpose and type of record; you can contact us for information about a specific record.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR’s conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your information. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity before handling a request.

You may complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, or your local supervisory authority.

Your choices and policy updates

You do not need to email us to browse the website. If you choose not to provide contact information, we may be unable to respond to an enquiry. We do not use this release to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. We will update this policy when our processing changes.
